🎀 Welcome to the World of Dreams & Entertainment! 🏰✨

Hello, everyone!!

I'm Cham, your accessibility-friendly guide to the world of dreams and entertainment! 🩷

Welcome to the magical world of Disney and the exciting adventures of Universal Studios Japan (USJ)! 🎠🌈

This website aims to help everyone enjoy theme parks in their own way by sharing information with accessibility in mind.

Whether you have low vision, are hard of hearing, or have different accessibility needs, everyone deserves the chance to enjoy the magic.

Let's discover new ways to enjoy entertainment together with Cham! 🎀✨

🌷 About This Website

🏰 Disney: Discover a world of dreams and magic!

🎢 Universal Studios Japan: Enjoy exciting adventures!

🌍 Multilingual Support: Available in Japanese, English, and Spanish!

♿ Accessibility: Working toward a website that is easier for everyone to use!

💖 Our Accessibility Commitment

We aim to create a website that considers readability, usability, and accessibility, so more people can enjoy entertainment.

We also plan to use axe-core, an open-source accessibility testing tool, to identify and improve potential accessibility issues.

🎀 A Message from Cham

Everyone deserves to experience the joy of the things they love.

We hope to create a world where people can enjoy entertainment in their own way, regardless of disability.
