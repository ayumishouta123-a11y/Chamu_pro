Introduction

Hello! I'm Chamu. I have a visual impairment and food allergies.

When visiting a theme park, I sometimes worry about finding my way around, reading menus, or enjoying attractions comfortably. This page summarizes accessibility services and food-allergy information published by Universal Studios Japan (USJ).

Services, eligibility requirements, and prices may change. Please check the official website before your visit.

Services for guests with visual impairments

USJ provides information about services that can help guests with visual impairments navigate the park and access information.

1. Navigation device for guests with visual impairments

  • Location: Guest Services, to the left of the park entrance.
  • A dedicated device is attached to the user's shoes and guides them toward a destination using vibrations felt through the feet.
  • Officially listed rental fee: ¥1,000 per device (tax-exempt).

2. Braille maps and tactile maps

  • Braille map: A map with Braille and printed text showing locations such as attractions and restaurants. Ask at Guest Services. You can contact the Information Center in advance if you need one before your visit.
  • Tactile map: Provides tactile information about key buildings and facilities in the park.

3. Support Sticker

Guests can wear a Support Sticker in a visible place, such as on their chest, so crew members know they may need assistance. Stickers are available at Guest Services. They do not provide priority admission to attractions.

If you need help finding a location or understanding information, ask at Guest Services or speak to a nearby crew member.

Guest Support Pass

Guests with a disability that makes waiting in line difficult and who have an eligible disability certificate may be able to use a Guest Support Pass.

How does it work?

It allows eligible guests to wait somewhere other than the regular queue during the attraction's wait time.

Where can I ask?

  • Guest Services
  • The entrance of an attraction

Please note: A Guest Support Pass is not a way to skip the queue or guarantee priority admission. Check the official rules and eligibility requirements before your visit.

Food allergies

Some USJ restaurants offer low-allergen set meals. Allergen information is also available for major menu items, covering 28 specified ingredients under Japan's food-labeling rules.

Low-allergen set meals

These meals are prepared without the following eight specified ingredients: eggs, milk, wheat, shrimp, crab, buckwheat, peanuts, and walnuts.

Before ordering

  1. Check the latest menu and restaurant information on the official website.
  2. Before entering the queue, ask a restaurant crew member for the latest allergen information.
  3. Review the information carefully and make your own final decision. Cross-contact may occur during food preparation.

A low-allergen meal is not necessarily safe for every allergy. Always check the current official information and speak with restaurant crew before ordering.

Discounted Studio Pass for guests with disabilities

USJ offers a discounted Studio Pass for eligible guests with disabilities.

CategoryReference price shown on the official ticket page
AdultFrom ¥4,700
ChildFrom ¥3,200

Prices may vary by date and sales conditions. The official purchase process may require the original eligible disability certificate or a supported digital certificate such as Mirairo ID. Check the current ticket page for accepted documents and requirements.

These are reference “from” prices, not a guaranteed price for every date. Confirm the current price and eligibility before purchasing.

Before your visit

  • Check the official accessibility guide and service locations.
  • If you have food allergies, review menu and allergen information in advance.
  • Charge your phone and prepare any apps you need.
  • Ask Guest Services or a crew member if you need help on the day.
  • Check official information again shortly before your visit because services and prices may change.

Conclusion

USJ provides various services, including navigation devices and Braille maps for guests with visual impairments, Guest Support Passes, and low-allergen meals at some restaurants.

Learning about the support available in advance and asking crew members questions may help make your visit less stressful.

I hope this guide is useful to anyone planning a visit to USJ!
